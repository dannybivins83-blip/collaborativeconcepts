The details

Questions,
answered.

How much can I actually make?+
Campaigns are estimated between $0.12 and $0.35 per verified mile, with up to 2,000 paid miles per month. More verified miles, busier corridors, visible parking, and bigger coverage all raise your rate. Every figure on this page is an estimate, not a guarantee — the sponsoring brand sets the rate per campaign.
How are miles verified?+
Each month you submit an odometer reading with a photo through your driver portal. We review and approve it before any payout — approved miles are what you get paid for. GPS device verification is available on a per-campaign basis when a sponsor funds it; it is not required for standard campaigns and is never on by default.
Will the wrap damage my paint?+
No — it protects it. Campaigns use paint-safe cast vinyl installed and removed by vetted professionals, and it shields the clear coat from UV fade and road rash while it's on.
Who pays for the wrap and removal?+
The sponsoring brand — design, install, and removal. Drivers never pay; early-exit terms are spelled out up front.
Do I choose the brand and design?+
Yes. Nothing touches your vehicle until you approve the sponsor, artwork, rate, and campaign length in writing. Declining an offer never affects future ones.
Can I do this with a leased or financed car?+
Financed — yes. Leased — usually; wraps aren't permanent modifications, but check your lease terms first.
Is the money taxable?+
Yes — sponsorship payouts are income (you'll receive a 1099 if you earn over the IRS threshold). Talk to your tax preparer; some drivers can also deduct a portion of vehicle expenses. We're not tax advisors, so get real advice for your situation.
How do referral fees work?+
Two tracks. Refer a driver: flat fees — $50 when they complete their first wrap, then $75 / $100 / $150 bonuses when they're still active at 3, 6, and 12 months (up to $375 per driver). Refer a sponsor: a percentage of their verified campaign spend — 10% of month one at launch, 5% at the 3-month mark, 5% at 6 months, and 10% at 12 months. Your referral just has to name you in the "Who referred you?" box when they sign up. Anyone can refer — you don't have to be an Adometr driver yourself.
What kinds of cars do brands want?+
Clean vehicles 2010 or newer, any make. High-mileage commuters on major corridors are gold. Light colors wrap best but any color works. Trucks, SUVs, and vans earn premium rates because of the larger canvas.
Can I wrap something that isn't a car — golf cart, trailer, boat?+
Yes. Golf carts and LSVs in cart-friendly downtowns and communities, box/work trailers, boats, and food trucks all take wraps beautifully — they just get paid differently. Since they don't rack up highway miles, sponsors pay a flat monthly placement fee based on where the asset lives and how many people see it. Pick your asset type on the application and we'll quote it that way.
