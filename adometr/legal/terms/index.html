1. What ADometr is

ADometr is a marketplace operated by Collaborative Concepts LLC (Lantana, Florida) that connects drivers with brands wanting vehicle-wrap advertising. We arrange the wrap, verify miles driven, and handle payment. Using this site or applying to drive means you accept these terms.

2. Who can drive

  • You are 18 or older with a valid driver’s license
  • You own or lease the vehicle, or have written permission to wrap it
  • You carry your own valid auto insurance and keep it active
  • Your vehicle is in sound cosmetic condition — wraps need clean, undamaged panels
  • Your information is truthful, including miles and routes

3. How payment works

Paid per verified mile

  • Each campaign states a per-mile rate and a monthly cap before you accept
  • Only verified miles are paid. Verification uses location data while the campaign is active — see the Privacy Policy
  • Payments issue monthly for the prior month’s verified miles
  • If verification is off or unavailable, those miles are not paid
  • You are paid as an independent contractor. We do not withhold taxes; you may receive a 1099 where required
