## RECITALS

A. The Company operates a marketplace that connects advertisers ("Sponsors") with
owners of private passenger vehicles who are willing to display removable vinyl
advertising material ("Wrap") on their vehicles in exchange for compensation.

B. Driver owns or lawfully leases the vehicle described in **Exhibit A** (the
"Vehicle") and wishes to display Wraps on the Vehicle as an independent contractor.

## 1. NATURE OF SERVICES — ADVERTISING DISPLAY ONLY

1.1 **Services.** Driver agrees to display a Wrap on the Vehicle and to operate the
Vehicle in the ordinary course of Driver's own personal and professional life. The
sole service Driver provides under this Agreement is **the display of advertising
material**. Driver is **not** engaged to drive, deliver, transport, or perform any
task for the Company or for any Sponsor.

1.2 **No driving directions.** Neither the Company nor any Sponsor directs, controls,
or supervises when, where, how, or whether Driver drives. Campaign Zone definitions
(Exhibit B) are **conditions of payment only** — they determine which miles are
compensable — and are not instructions to drive at any time, on any route, or in any
manner.

1.3 **No services for Sponsors.** Driver shall not perform errands, deliveries,
transportation, or any other services for a Sponsor, and shall not represent to any
person that Driver works for, is employed by, or acts on behalf of any Sponsor or the
Company.

## 2. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

2.1 Driver is an **independent contractor**. Nothing in this Agreement creates an
employment, agency, partnership, joint venture, or franchise relationship between
Driver and the Company, or between Driver and any Sponsor.

2.2 Driver has no authority to bind, act for, or make representations on behalf of
the Company or any Sponsor.

2.3 Driver is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on amounts
paid under this Agreement. The Company will issue IRS Form 1099-NEC where required.
No amounts will be withheld. Driver is not entitled to workers' compensation,
unemployment, health, or any other employee benefits.

2.4 Driver is free to pursue any other work or business, including for competitors of
the Company, except that Driver may not display advertising for a direct competitor
of an active Sponsor on the same Vehicle during that Sponsor's campaign.

## 3. DRIVER REPRESENTATIONS AND ONGOING REQUIREMENTS

Driver represents, and must maintain throughout the Term:

(a) A valid driver's license issued in Driver's own name;
(b) Vehicle registration for the Vehicle, current and valid;
(c) **Automobile insurance on the Vehicle in Driver's own name**, at no less than the
minimum coverage required by Florida law, and Driver is encouraged to carry bodily
injury liability coverage; Driver's insurance is and remains **primary** for any
incident involving the Vehicle;
(d) Lawful right to apply advertising material to the Vehicle (including under any
vehicle lease or finance agreement);
(e) A driving record free of DUI/DWI convictions within the past [7] years and free
of license suspension within the past [3] years;
(f) The Vehicle is model year [2010] or newer, in clean and roadworthy condition,
free of major body damage.

Driver must notify the Company within **48 hours** of: any accident involving the
Vehicle; any suspension or loss of license, registration, or insurance; any citation
for reckless driving or DUI; or any material damage to the Vehicle or the Wrap.

## 4. GIG-PLATFORM ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Driver acknowledges that some third-party platforms (including rideshare platforms)
maintain policies restricting exterior commercial advertising on vehicles used on
their platforms. **It is solely Driver's responsibility** to determine whether
displaying a Wrap complies with the policies of any platform Driver uses, and Driver
assumes all risk of any platform action (including deactivation). The Company makes
no representation that Wrap display is permitted by any third-party platform.

## 5. THE WRAP — INSTALLATION, CARE, OWNERSHIP, REMOVAL

5.1 **No cost to Driver.** Wrap production, professional installation, and end-of-
campaign professional removal are paid by the Company/Sponsor. **Driver pays nothing
to participate**, and the Company will never charge Driver a fee, deposit, or
"processing" charge of any kind.

5.2 **Approved installer only.** Installation and removal shall be performed only by
an installer designated by the Company. Driver shall make the Vehicle available at
the designated installer at reasonably scheduled times for installation, removal, and
any repair of the Wrap.

5.3 **Care of the Wrap.** Driver shall keep the Vehicle reasonably clean, shall not
alter, cover, or remove the Wrap, and shall follow the care instructions in Exhibit C
(hand wash; no automatic brush car washes; no pressure washing at close range).

5.4 **Photo check-ins.** Driver shall submit a dated photograph of each wrapped side
of the Vehicle [monthly], and upon reasonable request.

5.5 **Ownership.** The Wrap and all advertising content remain the property of the
Company and/or the Sponsor. Vinyl used is industry-standard removable film designed
not to damage factory paint in good condition; the Company is not responsible for
pre-existing paint defects revealed or worsened at removal, and Driver accepts this
risk as to non-factory or previously damaged paint disclosed in Exhibit A.

5.6 **Unauthorized removal.** If Driver removes or defaces the Wrap other than
through the designated installer, or fails to make the Vehicle available for removal
at campaign end, Driver is responsible for the Company's actual costs of Wrap
replacement or professional removal, and forfeits any unpaid mileage compensation for
the period of noncompliance. [Counsel: review enforceability / liquidated damages.]

## 6. COMPENSATION

6.1 **Per verified mile.** The Company shall pay Driver the per-mile rate stated in
the applicable Campaign Addendum (**Exhibit B**) for **Verified Miles** driven within
the Campaign Zone, up to a maximum of [2,000] compensable miles per calendar month.

6.2 **Verified Miles** means miles substantiated by the verification method stated in
Exhibit B (odometer photo submissions, mileage-app screenshots, or a Company-
designated GPS tracking method). Unverified miles are not compensable.

6.3 **Payment timing.** Verified Miles are paid [monthly] within [15] days after the
end of each calendar month, by [direct deposit / check / payment app].

6.4 **Falsification.** Any falsification of mileage, odometer readings, GPS data, or
photographs is grounds for immediate termination, forfeiture of unpaid compensation,
and recovery of amounts previously paid on falsified submissions.

6.5 **No guarantee.** Compensation depends on Driver's actual verified miles.
The Company makes **no guarantee of any minimum earnings**.

6.6 **Referral program.** Any referral bonuses are governed by the referral terms in
**Exhibit D** as posted and amended by the Company from time to time, and are payable
only while this Agreement is in good standing.

## 7. CONDUCT

7.1 Driver shall operate the Vehicle lawfully and shall not operate the Vehicle under
the influence of alcohol or drugs, while using a handheld device in violation of law,
or in a reckless manner.

7.2 While the Wrap is displayed, Driver shall not use the Vehicle in connection with
any unlawful activity or in any manner or location that a reasonable person would
consider obscene, hateful, or materially damaging to the reputation of the Sponsor or
the Company. [Counsel: scope/vagueness review.]

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until
terminated. Each campaign runs for the period stated in its Exhibit B.

8.2 **Termination by Driver.** Driver may terminate at any time on [14] days' written
notice. Driver's sole obligations on termination are to make the Vehicle available
for professional Wrap removal and to return any Company property.

8.3 **Termination by Company.** The Company may terminate at any time, with or
without cause, on written notice. If the Company terminates without cause mid-
campaign, Driver is paid for all Verified Miles through the removal date.

8.4 **Immediate termination.** The Company may terminate immediately for: breach of
Sections 3, 6.4, or 7; loss of license, registration, or insurance; or any accident
or conduct that materially damages the Wrap or a Sponsor's brand.

8.5 Sections 2, 5.5, 5.6, 9, 10, and 11 survive termination.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION; ASSUMPTION OF RISK

9.1 **Driver's vehicle, Driver's risk.** Driver operates the Vehicle at Driver's sole
risk. As between the Parties, Driver is solely responsible for the operation,
maintenance, and condition of the Vehicle and for compliance with all laws.

9.2 **Indemnity.** Driver shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Company, its
members and managers, and each Sponsor, from and against any third-party claims,
damages, liabilities, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out
of (a) Driver's operation or ownership of the Vehicle, including any accident or
traffic violation; (b) Driver's breach of this Agreement; or (c) Driver's negligence
or willful misconduct. This Section does not require Driver to indemnify any party
for that party's own negligence or willful misconduct. [Counsel: conform to Florida
indemnity law.]

9.3 **Insurance primary.** Driver's automobile insurance is primary as to any
incident involving the Vehicle. Nothing in this Agreement constitutes insurance.
