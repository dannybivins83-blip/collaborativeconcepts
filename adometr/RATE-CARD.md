## Terms
- 3-month minimum, month-to-month after · 10% discount on 12-month prepay
- Fleet pricing: 5+ cars −10%, 10+ cars −15% (media only, never production)
- 21-day replacement guarantee on any inactive vehicle (exclusive remedy)
- Monthly report: verified miles, photos, estimated impressions (estimates, always labeled)
- Effective CPM at typical mileage: **~$4–8 estimated** (partial, 1,500 mi/mo) down to
  **~$2–4** (full wrap at cap) vs $10–30 for static billboards — quote the range, never a promise
- Founding-advertiser rate: first 10 brands lock these prices for 12 months
- Anything custom below these numbers requires owner sign-off before it is sent
