01 — What we do

Four lines of business.

One operator, four connected verticals. Each is run as a standalone unit with its own economics — and each feeds the others.

Line 01

Coastal Development & Deal Structuring

Off-market coastal acquisitions and redevelopment. Investor JVs, wholesale and assignment structures — underwritten to a defined exit before we commit capital.

Line 02

Operation Solutions — Custom Software

We replace expensive SaaS stacks with software you own. One-time build, no per-seat license fees. A reference client cut $5,200/mo in recurring cost.

Line 03

Roof Consulting

Pre-listing inspection reports and HVHZ permit packets. Documentation that clears diligence and stands up to South Florida code — produced fast, priced clean.

Line 04

Florida Solar Exit

Helping homeowners out of failed and predatory solar financing — PACE / Ygrene loans that trapped equity and stalled sales. A clean path off the lien.
