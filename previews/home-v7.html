01 / Practice

Four lines. One operator.

Each vertical stands on its own economics. Together they compound — a development pipeline fed by consulting relationships, run on software we own outright.

01

Coastal Development & Deal Structuring

Off-market acquisitions, investor JVs, and wholesale / assignment structures on Florida coastal parcels. We source, underwrite, and structure the capital stack — then execute through a licensed GC partner.

02

Operation Solutions

Custom software that replaces expensive SaaS stacks. One-time build, no monthly license fees, full ownership. One reference client cut $5,200/mo in recurring software spend.

03

Roof Consulting

Pre-listing inspection reports and HVHZ permit packets for Florida property. Documentation that closes transactions and clears High-Velocity Hurricane Zone review.

04

Florida Solar Exit

Helping homeowners out of failed and predatory solar financing — PACE / Ygrene assessments that attach to the property. A dedicated platform guides the exit. solarexit.collaborativeconceptsfl.com ↗
