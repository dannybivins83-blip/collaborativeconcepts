The Thesis

Most operators do one thing. We built the whole stack around a single deal.

Real estate returns leak at the seams — between the finder and the builder, between the software vendor and the operator, between the homeowner and the paperwork. Collaborative Concept closes those seams by owning each discipline in-house or through aligned partners. When the deal, the construction, the tooling, and the back office answer to the same table, the margin stays in the deal.

01 — Development

Coastal development and off-market deal structuring — investor JVs, wholesale and assignment structures.

02 — Operation Solutions

Custom software that replaces expensive SaaS stacks — one-time build, no per-seat license fees.

03 — Roof Consulting

Pre-listing inspection reports and HVHZ permit packets, built to the standard South Florida demands.

04 — Florida Solar Exit

Helping homeowners out of failed or predatory solar loans — PACE and Ygrene financing entanglements.
