02 — Deal Architecture

The capital stack, plainly stated.

Capital sits senior to sweat. Senior bank debt anchors the base. The equity partner contributes 20–25% — cash or land — and holds second position. The developer carries execution and takes the back-end residual. Profit above cost and capital splits 50 / 50.
