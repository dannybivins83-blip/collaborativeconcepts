I · The thesis

Four lines of business, run by the same hands.

Collaborative Concept is a single Florida operation that refuses to specialize into irrelevance. It runs four disciplined lines of business, and the discipline is the point: real numbers, clean paperwork, named partners, and an operator who is accountable for every one of them. There are no hand-offs to explain away when a deal gets hard.

The four lines share a spine. Coastal development and off-market deal structuring is the front door — sourcing land that never hits the MLS and packaging the equity to build on it. Operation Solutions is the software arm: custom systems that replace an expensive SaaS stack with a one-time build the owner keeps. A reference client cut $5,200 a month in subscriptions. Roof consulting produces the pre-listing reports and HVHZ permit packets that keep Florida coastal transactions moving. And Florida Solar Exit pulls homeowners out of failed PACE and Ygrene solar loans that quietly encumber a title and stall a sale.

II · The flagship

A double lot in Madeira Beach, and a live raise.

The clearest way to understand how Collaborative Concept works is to watch it on one deal. On a double lot in Madeira Beach, the plan is a two-home coastal redevelopment. The land is identified. The build team is set. The equity is being structured now.

III · Who builds it

Named, licensed, and accountable — no vague "partners."

A raise is only as good as the people executing it, so here is exactly who holds the license, who swings the hammer, and who structures the equity. The general contractor is La Gala Construction, a DBA of Tilt Patchers, Inc., led by Keith La Gala with more than 25 years building in Florida — FL CGC 059211. Roofing runs through SeaBreeze Roofing & Sheet Metal, HVHZ-ready and permit-clean — FL CCC1328689 · CVC57073.

The development and equity arm is K5 Investment Group. The structure is deliberately plain: the equity partner contributes 20–25% in cash or land, takes 2nd position, and splits 50/50 on the back end across a 12–24 month hold. Collaborative Concept sponsors and executes. The capital stack below is the whole arrangement on one screen.
