What to have ready

None of this is required, but it makes the first call faster:

  • Rough linear footage, or just the property address so we can look at it.
  • Fence height you're after, and whether you have an HOA.
  • New install, replacement, or repair.
  • Any deadline you're working against — a closing date, a pool inspection, an HOA notice.

Emergencies and storm damage

If your fence is down and it's holding a dog in or serving as a pool barrier, say that when you call. Those don't wait in line.
