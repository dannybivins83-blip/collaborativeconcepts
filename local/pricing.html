The fine print, up front

Things we won't do, even if you ask

Some of these will cost us deals. We'd rather lose the deal than the licence — or hand you a fine with your invoice.

We won't buy, generate or gate your reviews.

Not fake ones, not "incentivised" ones, and we won't filter unhappy customers away from the review link. The FTC's Fake Reviews Rule has been enforceable since late 2025 with penalties around $51,744 per violation — and it's you they fine, not your marketing company. We'll get you real reviews by asking real customers. It's slower. It's also the only thing that survives.

We won't invent Google listings at addresses you don't occupy.

Fake or virtual-office listings are a well-worn trick for faking local coverage. Google suspends them, and it can take the rest of your profile down with them. If you want to rank in the next town over, we do it with a real page about real work, not a fake pin on a map.

We won't text people who didn't agree to be texted.

Every form we build captures written consent, records the exact wording someone agreed to, and honours STOP immediately. Unsolicited texts run $500–$1,500 per message under the TCPA, and the lawsuits land on the business whose name is on the text. Our forms are built so that's never you.

We won't guarantee you a #1 ranking.

Nobody controls Google's results, so nobody can promise you a position in them. Anyone who does is either lying or about to do something that gets you penalised. We'll show you what we can influence and report honestly on the rest.

Straight answers

The questions you'd ask on the phone

Why publish prices when nobody else does?
Because "request a quote" wastes both our time. You want to know if you can afford this before you spend forty minutes on a discovery call, and we'd rather talk to people who already know the number. If our pricing doesn't work for you, you've lost thirty seconds instead of an afternoon.
What does "exclusive" actually mean?
One business per trade, per town. If we run a water-damage site for Lantana and you're on it, we won't put a second Lantana water-damage company on it — not for more money, not ever. That's why we can only take a handful of clients per category, and why we'll sometimes tell you a slot is taken.
What happens if I stop paying?
Depends which thing you bought. Your Presence Kit site is yours — you own the domain and the code, and it keeps running whether or not you keep us. A lead-gen site is different: we build, own and rank that one, and you're renting exclusive access to what it produces. When you stop, the leads stop. We say that plainly up front because a lot of companies don't, and it's the single thing people are angriest about later.
Do I have to sign a contract?
You sign a simple agreement so we both know the scope — but there's no minimum term. Cancel any month, for any reason, and we won't invoice you for the privilege.
How fast does this work?
Honestly: ads work in days, a Google profile in weeks, rankings in months. Anyone promising page one in thirty days is selling you something. What we can promise is that you'll see something concrete in month one — usually the fixes from your audit, live.
Why are you cheaper than the agencies I've called?
No office, no account managers, no sales team taking a cut of your retainer. It's a small operation using a lot of automation, which means the work that used to take an agency a week takes us a day. You're paying for the result, not the overhead.
Do you work with businesses outside Palm Beach County?
Occasionally, but this is deliberately a local operation. Being able to drive to your shop is part of what makes it work.
