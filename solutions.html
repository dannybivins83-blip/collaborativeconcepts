Questions

The fine print, in plain language.

Do I actually own the software?+
Yes — outright. The app, the code, and the data are yours. No vendor lock-in, no being held hostage by a subscription you can't leave.
Are there monthly license fees?+
Never. It's a one-time build (or paid out of savings) — the entire point is to get you off recurring subscriptions and per-seat charges. The only optional ongoing cost is flat hosting/support if you want us to manage it.
How does it actually save me money?+
You stop paying for the 5–10 third-party apps and per-seat fees you're renting today. One custom app you own replaces them — our reference client cut $5,200 a month. For rentals, it also stops platforms from taking up to 15% of every rent check.
What about the apps I already pay for?+
Once your custom system is live and proven, you cancel them. We make sure your data comes with you and nothing breaks in the switch.
Can it be changed later?+
Yes — it's built to be easy to customize. A new field, form, or workflow doesn't mean a new contract or a forced upgrade tier like the SaaS apps charge for.
What's the "pay only what you save" option?+
We baseline your current software + admin cost from your own bills, build the replacement on our dime, and take a share of the measured savings for a set window — then it's fully yours. No savings, no fee.
What kinds of businesses is this for?+
Owner-operated businesses overpaying for software — trades, roofing, restoration, property management, rentals, and the SMBs around them. If a custom build won't clearly save you money, we'll tell you straight.
