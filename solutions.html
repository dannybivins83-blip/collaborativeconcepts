Questions

The fine print, in plain language.

How do you actually measure that it "worked"?add
We agree on one Single Metric of Record before any build — like admin hours, qualified appointments, or no-show rate. We baseline it from a trailing 3–6 month average pulled from your own system, you sign off on that number, and then we track baseline vs. live on a shared dashboard. Our fee comes only from the measured difference.
What if the number doesn't move?add
Then you owe us nothing beyond the hard software costs already covered. We finance our own time — that's our risk. No result, no invoice.
How do I know the result is from you and not something else?add
It's written into the agreement: the build is the material change during the measurement window, and you agree not to run a competing initiative on the same number. Where it's practical, we run a holdout — half the volume through the automation, half the old way — so the lift is undeniable.
What's the upfront cost, really?add
In our most common (Hybrid) structure, a small implementation fee that covers only hard third-party costs — API credits, hosting, messaging. That's it. We never bill you for our build time and we never finance your software bill indefinitely.
How fast is it live?add
Most builds reuse proven templates and go live in days, not weeks. We ship the smallest reliable thing that moves the number, then expand once it's proven.
What happens after the measurement window?add
The default 12-month window converts to a flat maintenance retainer to keep it running, or a one-time buyout for a perpetual license. You own your data the entire time.
What kinds of businesses is this for?add
Owner-operated small businesses with one clear, measurable bottleneck and clean data — trades, roofing, restoration, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and the SMBs around them. If your metric can't be cleanly measured, we'll tell you straight and decline rather than overpromise.
