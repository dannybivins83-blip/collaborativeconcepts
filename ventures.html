Venture & Sub-Site Policy

The role decides the treatment.

Every related property is assigned one corporate role first, and the role sets a ceiling on how much of it belongs on this site.

Venture disclosure. Ventures are operated separately from Collaborative Concept client engagements. A venture is not a client service, is not staffed or billed through an engagement, and is never a condition of working with the firm.

Naming a property here is not a claim of sole ownership, control or a licensed role.

Why ventures stay separate

Each venture operates under its own brand and structure so we can protect our partners, serve the right audience and build with focus.

  • Clearer customer promise

    Distinct brands and messaging that speak directly to each audience.

  • Compliance boundary

    Independent operations with the right licences, records and service scopes.

  • Focused experience

    Teams, tools and roadmaps aligned to each venture's mission and customers.
