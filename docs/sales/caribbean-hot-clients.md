# Caribbean Hot-Client Target List — Façade Access, Anchor Certification & Inspection

> **What this is.** A sales-ready dossier of the 10 hottest Caribbean prospects for
> our building-access + inspection business (davit/anchor systems, swing stages,
> OSHA 1910.27 anchor certification & annual recert, GPR/NDT façade investigation,
> concrete restoration), plus channel multipliers and a bench. Compiled 2026-07-14
> from five parallel research sweeps (Bermuda/Bahamas, Cayman/Turks, PR/USVI,
> Dutch/East Caribbean, and portfolio/channel plays).
>
> **How to use.** Every prospect has WHO (buyer role), CONTACT (publicly listed
> business lines only — **verify by phone before any mail-merge**; several corporate
> sites block scraping and some numbers came from directory listings), WHAT/WHERE,
> WHEN/WHY NOW (the trigger), HOW (the pitch), and PRICING (deal-size estimate).
>
> **Our edge, in one line:** most of these islands have **no local provider** for
> anchor load-testing, façade NDT, or PE-stamped certification — repeated searches
> for local rope-access/anchor-testing firms across the DR and Dutch Caribbean
> returned zero. US owners, hotel flags, and their insurers import OSHA-equivalent
> compliance offshore; salt air destroys galvanized hardware in ~1 year, so our
> **all-stainless spec + fly-out recert program** is the differentiator.
