# International (non-US) Target List — Façade Access, Anchor Certification & Inspection

> **What this is.** The non-US expansion dossier for the building-access + inspection business
> (davit/anchor systems, swing stages, anchor certification & annual recert, GPR/NDT façade
> investigation, concrete restoration, PE-stamped engineering). Compiled 2026-07-17 from six
> parallel research sweeps: Panama, Mexico (Caribbean coast), Mexico (Pacific + CDMX),
> Colombia + Central America, the Gulf (UAE/Qatar), and coastal Brazil. Companion to
> `caribbean-hot-clients.md`.
>
> **How to use.** Contacts are publicly listed business/department lines — **verify by phone
> before any mail-merge**; several corporate sites block scraping, and inferred email patterns
> are flagged. Deal sizes are estimates, not quotes.
>
> **The core wedge abroad (US OSHA does NOT apply outside the US):** lead with (1) **US-standard
> PE-stamped engineering + independent third-party certification** the local market can't
> self-produce, (2) **marine-grade / 316 stainless** for salt-air corrosion, (3) **hotel
> brand-standard** (Marriott/Hilton/Hyatt/Accor/Four Seasons/Ritz) + insurer compliance, and
> (4) **construction-phase embeds** on the wave of towers going vertical now (5–10× cheaper than
> retrofit). Local labor laws (Mexico NOM-009-STPS-2011; Colombia Res. 4272/2021; Brazil
> NR-35/NR-18/NBR-16325) add a recurring-recert hook.
