# Gulf & Brazil — Partner / Reactivation Playbook (not cold email)

> **Why this is separate.** Unlike the green-field markets (Panama, Mexico, Colombia, Central
> America) where you lead with a cold install/cert pitch, the Gulf and Brazil are **mature,
> locally-regulated markets with entrenched incumbents.** Cold "we do it cheaper/better"
> outreach structurally loses here. This is the *partner, supply, and reactivate* plan.

---

## GULF (UAE + Qatar)

### The honest read
- **Equipment shipping from Florida → hard.** Local houses (TRANSWILL, VAPTEC/Facade Hoists, Sky Steel, Alimak-FacadeXS, Cox Gomyl, Manntech) hold stock, spares, service crews, and Dubai-Municipality-approved designs. US gear adds freight + no local service arm.
- **Routine third-party cert → gated.** Dubai mandates 3rd-party cert (a contractor won't hang a cradle without it), but only from **EIAC/ENAS-accredited** labs (Aries, SGS, Bureau Veritas, TÜV, Aqualis, DUTEST, GTS). A US/LEEA cert is **not locally accredited** → you can't win routine DM-compliance cert head-on.
- **The three lanes you can actually win:** (1) **Qatar reactivation**, (2) a **UAE channel/PE partnership**, (3) **coastal-corrosion engineering behind a locally-accredited partner** for owner-side verification.

## BRAZIL

### The honest read
- **Stainless anchors are table stakes, not a differentiator.** **Hook Engenharia** and **Ankerseg** already manufacture INOX 316 anchors/lifelines with civil-engineer ART, INMETRO testing, 100% pull-out testing — clustered in **SP/RJ/MG/RS**.
- Rope-access/alpinismo firms (ESL, Hawk, Climb, Tecnorope) own routine façade labor cheaply.
- **Regulatory demand is real and recurring:** NR-35 / NR-18 / NBR 16325 (certified anchor points + annual recert on every façade job), **Fortaleza's mandatory building-inspection law** (Lei 9.913/2012), and **Recife's "edifícios-caixão"** collapse risk.

### Where you win (four lanes)
1. **Trophy super-towers (45–52-story oceanfront new-builds)** — permanent **BMU/monorail** engineering + coastal-grade stainless embeds, where rope access is impractical and local permanent-system supply is thin.
   - **Diagonal / Colmeia** (Fortaleza Beira Mar/Mucuripe) — (85) 4006-1400 · diagonal.com.br
   - **Moura Dubeux** (NE incorporadora, design-stage embeds) — mouradubeux.com.br/fale-conosco
2. **GPR/NDT + PE-stamped concrete restoration** on aging coastal stock (bigger ticket, fewer local specialists).
   - **Marvan / Negócios** (Recife Boa Viagem, caixão risk) — `atendimento@marvanadm.com.br` · (81) 3128-7101
   - **Hotel Gran Marquise** (Fortaleza oceanfront) — (85) 4006-5000 (ask for Engenharia)
3. **The administradora governance layer** — sell a standardized, PE-stamped, independently-certified compliance program across a portfolio (reduces the síndico's liability — something one-off installers don't package).
   - **APSA** (national, Rio/NE/SP) — `atendimento@apsa.com.br` · (21) 3233-3000 — **the single biggest breadth play**
   - **Lello** (São Paulo) — `atendimento.condominios@lello.com.br`
4. **Geographic wedge — the Nordeste coast (CE/PE/BA/AL)** is under-served by the strongest local anchor makers (who cluster in SP/RJ/South). Prioritize **Fortaleza and Recife.**

### The partner move
- **Hook Engenharia / Ankerseg** (hookeng.com.br / ankerseg.com.br) — approach as **partner + supplier**, not competitor: supply coastal-grade swing stages/davits/specialty rigging into the Nordeste (where they don't operate), and co-bid trophy towers needing full BMU/monorail engineering or US-standard certification beyond their scope.

### What NOT to do in Brazil
- Don't compete on commodity anchor points or routine rope-access labor — you lose on price.
- Don't lead with "stainless" as the hook (locals already do INOX 316) — lead with **BMU-scale permanent systems, NDT/restoration depth, US PE credibility, and the governance layer.**

### Brazil sequencing
1. **APSA** (governance-layer breadth) + **Diagonal/Moura Dubeux** (trophy embeds) first.
2. **Gran Marquise + Marvan** for the NDT/restoration ticket in the Nordeste.
3. **Partner with Hook/Ankerseg** to co-serve trophy towers and own the Nordeste supply gap.

## One-line summary
**Gulf:** reactivate Qatar, sign a UAE channel partner, sell coastal engineering behind a local stamp. **Brazil:** win trophy-BMU + NDT/restoration + the Nordeste gap + the administradora governance layer; partner Hook/Ankerseg on the rest. In both, **partner and supply — don't cold-compete on commodity work.**
