# Photography & Illustration - Buy List

Authorised by OVERLORD (decision D2b). This is a **shoot / stock purchase brief**, not a task list.

## Why this exists

Every image on the site was cropped out of the design handoff's composed page mockups. The
entire master mockup is **864px wide for the whole page**, so the crops came out between
142px and 524px. They were then upscaled 3x with LANCZOS and unsharp, which is why the files
on disk now report 900-1500px.

**That reported size is not real detail.** Genuine captured detail is roughly one third of the
stated dimensions - the "real detail today" column below. The handoff contains no original
assets; the re-sent package was byte-identical (same MD5). Upscaling is maxed out and no
further processing will improve it.

## How to read "deliver at"

Slot render width at a 1440px viewport, doubled for 2x displays. Meeting that number with
**real captured pixels** is the requirement.

## Shooting notes

- **Palette.** Warm ivory, deep navy, sea-glass teal for Development, muted copper for
  Solutions. Avoid cool blue-grey casts - they fight the ivory ground.
- **Light.** Natural, directional, slightly warm. No hard flash, no HDR halos.
- **Development subjects.** Real Florida coastal property, vacant lots, framing, site work,
  aerials. Elevated and piling construction reads as authentic to this market.
- **Solutions subjects.** Screens showing real interfaces, drawings, permit packets, working
  desks. Shoot slightly off-axis so the screen is legible without being a flat screenshot.
- **Aspect.** Shoot wider than the listed aspect and leave crop room top and bottom.
- **Format.** Full-resolution originals plus 2x web exports, WebP or high-quality JPEG.

## Hard constraints

1. **No people without a signed release.** Several concept mockups show figures at conference
   tables. There are no approved portraits and no releases, so all of those slots currently use
   drafting linework. A photo with an identifiable person cannot go up without a model release.
2. **No third-party branding.** The concept imagery included visible signage from other
   companies. Replacements must be clear of it.
3. **Solutions proof panel: no figures in frame.** `CONTENT_AND_CLAIMS.md` forbids publishing
   the generated savings claim. Do not shoot or generate a screen showing revenue, savings,
   percentages or counts for that band - visible numbers would reintroduce the exact claim
   that was stripped.
4. **Nothing that asserts an unverified project.** A photo captioned as a specific named
   project becomes a claim about that project.

## The 10 drawing slots should not be photographed

The `.png` slots are technical linework - site plans, workflow schematics, illustrations.
These should be **redrawn as SVG**, not shot. Vector is resolution-independent, ends this
problem permanently for those slots, and matches the drafting language the design system
already uses. Cheaper than a shoot and strictly better.
