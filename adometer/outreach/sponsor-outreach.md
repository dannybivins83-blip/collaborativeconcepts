**1. "How do I know it works?"**

I'll be straight with you: impressions are estimates from industry benchmarks, not guarantees — but verified miles and code redemptions are hard numbers, which is more than any billboard has ever given you.

**2. "What if the driver crashes?"**
Your contract is with Collaborative Concepts LLC d/b/a Adometer — never with the driver. Drivers are independent contractors carrying their own insurance, which is primary, and the agreement walls your brand off from any of that. You're buying ad space, same as a billboard company's client isn't liable when a board blows over.

**3. "Why not just wrap my own van?"**

One van markets where you already are; a fleet markets where you aren't yet.
