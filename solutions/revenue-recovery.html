What this engagement is — and is not. The sprint delivers a defined process and a defined set of deliverables: the diagnostic, the ranked lists, the messaging, the cadence, the scorecard and the final review described on this page.

It does not include or imply a guaranteed revenue outcome. What follows depends on your market, your pricing, your capacity and how consistently your team runs the process once it is handed over. We publish no recovery percentages and make no earnings claims.

Collaborative Concept is not acting as your broker, attorney, accountant or contractor in this engagement.
