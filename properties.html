Roof Consulting

Independent roof assessments for buyers, sellers, insurance disputes, and code-compliance situations. We deliver an attorney-grade inspection report with measurements, photo evidence, NOA compliance notes, and remediation scope — the same documentation format we built for the off-market structure on 2820 NE 44th St, Lighthouse Point — see our Lighthouse Point waterfront real estate deep dive. Licensed remediation work performed by our partner SeaBreeze Roofing & Sheet Metal (FL CCC1328689 · CVC57073).

  • Insurance claim documentation & reinspection support

Consulting Practice
FLORIDA SOLAR EXIT
Failed solar loans · PACE / Ygrene · Predatory installers

Florida Solar Exit Program

A dedicated consulting practice for Florida homeowners trapped in failed, abandoned, or predatory residential solar loans (PACE, Ygrene, bankrupt installers, systems that never got PTO). We document the fraud or failure in an attorney-grade inspection report, refer to vetted Florida consumer-protection counsel, and coordinate licensed removal and roof remediation through SeaBreeze Roofing & Sheet Metal. Live at solarexit.collaborativeconceptsfl.com.

What's included
  • Paid inspection report formatted for attorneys
  • Self-help accountability playbook (lender, installer, AHJ, AG)
  • Attorney referrals to vetted FL consumer-protection counsel
  • Licensed system removal + roof remediation (SeaBreeze)
