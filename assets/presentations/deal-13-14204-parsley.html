Investment Thesis

Deal #13 — 14204 E Parsley Dr
Why This Deal
1

Rare Assemblage

1.16 acres is the largest available residential parcel on Madeira Beach island. Parcels this size almost never come to market.

2

$12/SF Land Cost

Individual lots in Madeira Beach trade at $50-$124/sf. Buying at $12/sf creates massive value through subdivision.

3

Multiple Exit Strategies

Sell subdivided lots individually, build spec homes on all lots, or a hybrid approach. Flexibility reduces risk.

4

Build-to-Rent Option

7 new construction homes at $3,500-$4,500/mo each = $294K-$378K annual rental income. Strong hold strategy alternative.

5

Island Scarcity

Madeira Beach is a barrier island with finite land. Every lot subdivided here captures irreplaceable island real estate value.

6

La Gala Vertical Play

CC/La Gala can build all 7 homes in-house, capturing both developer and contractor margin on each unit.

Proposed Development
  • 7 lots at ~6,500-7,200 sf each
  • Internal access road with utilities
  • Each lot supports 2,200-2,400 sf home
  • FEMA-compliant elevated construction
  • Consistent coastal architectural theme
  • Shared tropical landscaping package
  • Phase 1: Entitlement & platting (4-6 months)
  • Phase 2: Infrastructure (3-4 months)
  • Phase 3: Lot sales or vertical construction
  • Each home: 3-4 bed / 2-3 bath
  • Impact windows, metal roof standard
  • Individual home exit: $750K-$950K each
Due Diligence Checklist
  • Confirm subdivision feasibility with Pinellas County
  • Verify minimum lot size requirements
  • Environmental / wetlands assessment
  • Confirm utility capacity for 7 homes
  • Traffic impact study (if required)
  • Stormwater management plan
  • Title search & survey
  • Confirm BFE with FEMA flood map
