Investment Thesis

Deal #52 — Gulf Blvd Commercial
Why This Deal
1

Irreplaceable Location

Gulf Boulevard is the main commercial corridor on Madeira Beach. No new commercial land exists on the barrier island — this is a permanent supply advantage.

2

Tourist Traffic Engine

Madeira Beach draws millions of visitors annually. Gulf Blvd captures that foot and vehicle traffic directly, driving retail revenue year-round.

3

Multi-Tenant Diversification

Multiple retail units mean no single-tenant risk. If one tenant vacates, the remaining tenants continue producing income during re-leasing.

4

Below Replacement Cost

At $233/sf, this is significantly below the cost to build new commercial on the beach. Land alone would cost $150+/sf before construction.

5

Value-Add Upside

Cosmetic improvements and strategic re-leasing can push rents 15-20% above current levels, driving NOI growth and appreciation.

6

Cash Flow + Appreciation

This is a dual-return play: immediate cash flow from rents plus long-term appreciation as beach community property values continue rising.
