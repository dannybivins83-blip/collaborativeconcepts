How to read this list

A register, not a highlight reel.

Division

Which side of the business the entry sits under — Development, Solutions or Ventures. Ventures are tracked apart from client work.

Status

Where the work stands today, stated plainly and updated as it changes. A status describes a present condition, never a result.

Scope

The part of the program Collaborative Concept is responsible for. Design, engineering, survey and legal work is performed by the licensed professionals engaged for it.

No performance figures, valuations, returns or completion claims are published on this page. Nothing here is an offer to sell or a solicitation of an investment.
