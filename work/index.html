What each entry records.

Selected Work is a register, not a highlight reel. An entry earns a line here when there is real work behind it — a parcel being planned, a system being built, a position being managed — and it says only what is true today.

Where a project needs design, engineering, survey, brokerage, or legal work, that work is performed by the licensed professionals engaged for it. Collaborative Concept coordinates the program and stays accountable for the parts it owns.
