Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-24

The short version

Redlight is local-first. Your camera feed, location, and stop history live on your device and are never transmitted to us. Anonymous usage counts may be sent to our analytics provider so we can improve the product.

What lives where

DataWhereWhy
Camera framesOn-device only. Not recorded, not stored, not transmitted.Detect green lights.
LocationOn-device only. Reduced to "stops" (intersection + duration).Detect stops.
Streak / preferencesOn-device AsyncStorage.Persist your progress.
Anonymous eventsAnalytics provider. No identifiers, no location.Improve the product.

What we don't collect

  • Name, email, phone — Redlight has no account.
  • The contents of your camera feed.
  • Raw GPS coordinates of any stop.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, calendar.

Permissions

Camera (Camera Mode only), Location (Auto-Track only), Motion (in-vehicle vs. on-foot filter), Notifications (daily streak reminder). Revoke any of these in your device settings at any time.

Your choices

Clear local data: Settings → Reset → Clear All Data, or uninstall. Disable analytics: Settings → Privacy (coming soon).
