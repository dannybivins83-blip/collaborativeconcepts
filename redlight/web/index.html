Four pillars.

One feeling: the next time you sit at a red, you'll feel the meter running.

01

The math.

A lifetime time-tax calculator. Move six sliders. See the number you spend at reds. Then see the chunk you can take back.

02

Auto-track.

GPS detects every stop without you opening the app. Reviews your drive in one glance. Attentive or lost — no gray area.

03

The streak.

One per day. Miss a day, lose the streak. A small, daily ritual of paying attention. Achievements quietly stack up.

04

Camera mode.

Mount your phone. The camera watches the light. When it turns green, the app chimes — only if you don't accelerate. The app never asks you to look at it.
