The Wrap Works For You

It's not just an ad — it's armor for your paint

Protects the factory finish

Cast vinyl shields your clear coat from UV fade, light road rash, bird droppings, and minor scuffs. Under the wrap, your paint stays showroom-fresh — which helps resale value.

100% removable

Campaign ends or you sell the car? A professional installer peels the wrap with zero paint damage. Wraps are the same tech dealerships use on their own inventory.

Installed & insured by pros

We only use vetted, certified wrap shops. Installation, maintenance guidance, and end-of-campaign removal are all part of the sponsorship — never out of your pocket.

Questions, Answered

Frequently asked questions

How much can I actually make?
It depends on the campaign, but the levers are simple: more verified miles, higher-traffic routes (I-95, Turnpike, dense city streets), visible daytime parking, and bigger wrap coverage all raise your rate. Typical full-wrap sponsorships in metro markets run a few hundred dollars a month — use the calculator above for a personalized estimate, then apply to see real offers.
Will the wrap damage my paint?
No — it protects it. Professional cast vinyl acts as a sacrificial layer over your clear coat, shielding it from sun fade, light debris, and scratches. When a certified installer removes it at campaign end, the paint underneath is typically in better shape than the rest of the car would have been.
Who pays for the wrap and removal?
The sponsoring brand covers design, professional installation, and end-of-campaign removal. Drivers never pay anything. If you leave a campaign early, the campaign agreement spells out the pro-rated terms up front — no surprises.
How do you verify my mileage?
A lightweight app (or plug-in GPS device) logs your driving. It's how brands trust the miles and how you get credit for premium corridors — the same trip on I-95 at rush hour is worth more than the same distance on an empty back road. Location data is used for campaign reporting only.
Can I do this with a leased or financed car?
Financed — yes, it's your car; the wrap is removable and paint-safe. Leased — usually yes for the same reason, but check your lease terms first; most leases only prohibit permanent modifications, which a vinyl wrap is not. Rideshare and delivery drivers are especially good fits because of their mileage.
Do I get to approve the design and the brand?
Always. You see the exact brand, design mock-up on your vehicle model, rate per mile, and campaign length before accepting. Decline anything you're not comfortable driving — it never affects future offers.
Is the money taxable?
Yes — sponsorship payouts are income (you'll receive a 1099 if you earn over the IRS threshold). Talk to your tax preparer; some drivers can also deduct a portion of vehicle expenses. We're not tax advisors, so get real advice for your situation.
How do referral fees work?
Two tracks. Refer a driver: flat fees — $50 when they complete their first wrap, then $75 / $100 / $150 bonuses when they're still active at 3, 6, and 12 months (up to $375 per driver). Refer a sponsor: a percentage of their verified campaign spend — 10% of month one at launch, 5% at the 3-month mark, 5% at 6 months, and 10% at 12 months. Your referral just has to name you in the "Who referred you?" box when they sign up. Anyone can refer — you don't have to be a WrapMiles driver yourself.
What kinds of cars do brands want?
Clean vehicles 2010 or newer, any make. High-mileage commuters on major corridors are gold. Light colors wrap best but any color works. Trucks, SUVs, and vans earn premium rates because of the larger canvas.
Can I wrap something that isn't a car — golf cart, trailer, boat?
Yes. Golf carts and LSVs in cart-friendly downtowns and communities, box/work trailers, boats, and food trucks all take wraps beautifully — they just get paid differently. Since they don't rack up highway miles, sponsors pay a flat monthly placement fee based on where the asset lives and how many people see it (a golf cart cruising a busy downtown or a trailer parked on a visible corner can out-earn a low-mileage car). Pick your asset type on the application and we'll quote it that way.
