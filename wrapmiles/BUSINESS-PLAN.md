# WrapMiles — Business Plan
**Entity:** WrapMiles LLC (to be formed; Collaborative Concepts venture) · **Owner:** Danny Bivins
**Prepared:** 2026-07-28 (roundtable-stress-tested, Full C-Suite preset) · **Year-1 cash budget: $5k–$15k**

---

## 1. The Business

WrapMiles is a two-sided marketplace for **pay-per-mile mobile out-of-home advertising** in
South Florida. Local brands pay to have their design professionally wrapped on everyday
drivers' vehicles; drivers earn $0.12–$0.35 per verified mile (capped 2,000 paid mi/mo);
WrapMiles keeps the spread and owns the sponsor relationship, verification, and reporting.
Non-car assets (golf carts, trailers, boats, food trucks) rent at flat monthly placement rates.

**Why now / why us:** the category is proven nationally (Wrapify 250k+ drivers, Carvertise,
Nickelytics) but the nationals ignore sub-$50k local budgets. Danny's construction/roofing/HOA
network IS the initial sponsor market, and the entire platform — marketing site, driver/sponsor/
admin portals, referral engine, verification pipeline, contracts — is **already built and live**
at collaborativeconceptsfl.com/wrapmiles at ~zero marginal cost.

## 2. Market

- **Demand (advertisers):** South Florida SMBs already buying OOH/radio/PPC — home services,
  PI law, med spas, gyms, restaurants, franchises — plus public-sector district marketers
  (DDAs/CRAs) and a flagship pursuit (Brightline). Static I-95 billboards run $1,500–$3,000+/mo
  at ~$10–30 CPM; WrapMiles lands at an estimated $1–4 effective CPM.
- **Supply (drivers):** gig drivers, home-health nurses, pool/pest/lawn route operators,
  outside sales reps, construction subs — high-daytime-mileage populations concentrated on the
  Jupiter→Miami I-95 corridor. Supply acquisition is referral-led ($375/driver ladder).

## 3. Unit Economics (per average car, partial wrap)

| Line | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Sponsor media fee (pricing guardrail: 2.0–2.5× driver cost; floor $750 full wrap) | ~$600–675 |
| Driver payout (1,500 verified mi × $0.18) | ~$270 |
| **Gross margin per car** | **~$330–405 (≈55%)** |

One-time wrap production+install (~$800–1,500 partial) is billed to the sponsor at cost +15%
and **collected before production starts** — wraps are never fronted on spec (CFO condition).
Verification cost ladder: odometer photos $0 → Smartcar ~$3/car → GPS device ~$9/car (only on
campaigns whose sponsors pay for corridor-level proof).

## 4. Go-To-Market (in order)

1. **Own network first (month 1):** roofers, PI attorneys, med spas, property managers Danny
   already knows. Target: first signed pilot (3–5 cars) from a warm intro. Danny's own truck
   becomes demo wrap #1 (fits budget; rolling sales asset).
2. **DDA/CRA pilot (months 1–3):** 3–5-car district campaign priced under small-purchase
   threshold; public-agency logo becomes the credibility anchor. Pitch letter is written.
3. **Referral flywheel (continuous):** every driver is a recruiter ($375 ladder, links live);
   agencies/chambers earn 10/5/5/10% of sponsor spend for introductions.
4. **Scout agent cadence:** dedicated scout session works both pipelines; outreach packs and
   objection sheets are written. Standing goals: 50 wrap-ready drivers corridor-wide;
   3 paying sponsors, then $25k MRR.

## 5. Financial Projection (base case, conservative)

| Milestone | Cars live | Gross media/mo | Driver payouts/mo | WrapMiles gross/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M3 (first pilot + 1) | 5 | ~$3.2k | ~$1.4k | ~$1.8k |
| M6 (3 sponsors) | 12 | ~$7.8k | ~$3.4k | ~$4.4k |
| M12 (8–10 sponsors) | 30 | ~$19.5k | ~$8.5k | ~$11k |

Year-1 revenue ≈ $80–120k at ~55% gross margin before owner time. Break-even on cash is
month 2–3 (fixed costs are trivial: LLC $125, attorney ~$1k, GL insurance ~$1k/yr, hosting $0).
Downside case (revenue 30% under): still cash-positive — the model has no fixed burn; the real
cost of underperformance is Danny's time, not capital.

## 6. Organization

- **Owner/CEO:** Danny — sells sponsors, approves drivers, signs contracts.
- **Automation bench:** admin portal (matchmaking/approvals/ledger), scout agent
  (pipeline both sides), OVERLORD (infra/keys), build agent (this repo). No hires year 1.
- **First hire trigger:** >25 active cars or >6 active sponsors → part-time ops coordinator
  (mileage review, installer scheduling, driver comms).

## 7. Risks & Mitigations

1. **Two-sided cold start** → sell demand first; wrap nothing until a sponsor pays; 3× wrap-ready
   driver bench per campaign keeps supply ahead of signed demand.
2. **Attribution skepticism (biggest sales objection)** → promo codes/QR on every wrap, verified-
   mile reports, "estimates clearly labeled" honesty as brand position.
3. **Owner bandwidth (biggest real risk — flagged by HR/CEO):** WrapMiles competes with La Gala's
   pipeline for Danny's hours. Mitigation: hard cap ~5 hrs/wk until first sponsor cash clears;
   scout agent + portals do the administrative lifting; kill-review at Day 90 if no signed pilot.
4. **Liability / driver incident** → LLC isolation, driver-primary insurance + indemnity,
   sponsor no-control clause (contracts drafted, attorney review pending), GL policy before
   first wrap.
5. **Rideshare platform policies** → target delivery/route drivers first; rideshare risk sits
   with driver by contract; never market "Uber-approved."
6. **Driver churn mid-campaign** → 21-day replacement guarantee priced into margin; bench depth.

## 8. Milestones & Kill Criteria

- **Aug 2026:** LLC filed, GL bound, contracts attorney-approved, demo truck wrapped,
  10 warm sponsor pitches delivered.
- **Sep 2026:** first signed IO with production fee collected; 15 wrap-ready drivers.
- **Oct 2026 (Day 90 kill-review):** if no signed sponsor → stop outbound spend, keep site live
  as passive lead catcher, revisit quarterly. If signed → scale per GTM.
- **Q4 2026:** 3 paying sponsors, 12+ cars, Smartcar verification live, DDA pilot submitted.
- **2027:** $25k MRR target; evaluate ops hire and Brightline/agency channel.
