The Range House

Pensacola, FL · 13.5 acres · Adaptive reuse

An all-weather group-getaway destination — built around golf simulators. A private, bookable lodge of group suites wrapped around eight sim bays, a bar, and an event room, sold to the traveling groups this market already attracts. An adaptive reuse of an existing 40,688 SF building on 13.5 acres in Pensacola's growing I-110 corridor, with our licensed GC partner, Legally Construction.

The Concept

Three businesses under one roof.

Part Airbnb, part entertainment venue, part corporate retreat — sold to groups who want one roof, an all-weather activity, and a buy-the-whole-place experience.

  • ~24 group suites (sleeps ~112 at full buyout)
  • 8 golf-simulator bays — play day or night, rain or shine
  • Bar, lounge & catering-ready event space
  • Outdoor skate park — mini ramp & street plaza on the grounds
  • Adaptive reuse — a lower-cost basis than ground-up
The Building · Clubhouse Meets the Coast

A golf clubhouse with a Gulf Coast beach feel.

The Range House blends a Southern golf clubhouse with easy Gulf Coast beach style — deep verandahs and a cupola, white coastal materials and a metal roof, palms and water and greens all around. Real clubhouses and coastal-Florida buildings below — the design target for the adaptive-reuse skin, not the finished building.

On-Site Activity · The Grounds

And a skate park on the grounds.

Out on the Phase 2 balance-of-site, beside the event lawn, a compact skate park adds one more anytime draw — a mini ramp for transition plus street obstacles (rails and ledges) for the crew that skates. Another reason to book the whole place, and a magnet for younger groups, families and events.

Reference photography of comparable skate parks — illustrative of the amenity, not the property. Photos via Pexels. Siting shown conceptually in the Phase 2 balance-of-site (see C-1.0).

Why Pensacola · Why This Site

Demand that already drives here.

Pensacola pulls golf trips, military families, corporate teams, and work crews year-round — and the corridor is on the rise.

  • Golf-trip destination — the sim beats rain, heat & nightfall
  • NAS Pensacola — 53,000+ jobs; year-round, counter-seasonal demand
  • Aerospace growth — ST Engineering MRO, +1,700 jobs in this corridor
  • Navy Federal (~10,000 employees), beaches & the airport, minutes away

Ancillary Revenue · Guest Perk

Order in — and the house earns on every order.

Groups staying at The Range House order food straight to their suite from the delivery apps they already use. Each one runs through the venue's partner referral link — so The Range House earns a bonus on every order, and every tap is tracked in the operator dashboard. Zero added labor, pure ancillary margin on top of lodging and sim play.
