The Range House

1995 Hollywood Ave, Pensacola · 13.5 acres · Adaptive reuse

An all-weather group-getaway destination — built around golf simulators. A private, bookable lodge of group suites wrapped around eight sim bays, a bar, and an event room, sold to the traveling groups this market already attracts. An adaptive reuse of an existing 40,688 SF building on 13.5 acres in Pensacola's growing I-110 corridor, with our licensed GC partner, Legally Construction.

Ancillary Revenue · Guest Perk

Order in — and the house earns on every order.

Groups staying at The Range House order food straight to their suite from the delivery apps they already use. Each one runs through the venue's partner referral link — so The Range House earns a bonus on every order, and every tap is tracked in the operator dashboard. Zero added labor, pure ancillary margin on top of lodging and sim play.
