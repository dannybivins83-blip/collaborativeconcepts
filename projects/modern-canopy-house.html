Preserving the lines that make the building.

Mid-century modern buildings are defined by horizontal sight lines, cantilevered slabs, and floor-to-ceiling glass. Rebuild them wrong and the whole thing reads as generic. Our licensed GC partner restored Springbrook's slab edges, railings, and waterproofing without compromising the original proportions — the kind of detail work investors see every time we pitch a coastal redevelopment with "fit in and add to the community" in the mission statement.

Scope of work

  • Exterior concrete restoration
  • Slab edge & railing repair
  • Waterproofing & caulking
  • Board & resident coordination
  • Mid-century detail preservation
