The Opportunity

A non-conforming double-lot assembled and entitled for residential development.

Two contiguous lots on a waterfront street in Madeira Beach, on Florida's Gulf coast. Planned as one project rather than two, the site supports elevated coastal residences designed inside the envelope the site actually allows.

Risk Factors

We manage these actively and carry contingency in the plan rather than hope for it.

Permitting & entitlementsAgency review timing sits outside our control.Approval-dependent work is sequenced early with experienced local consultants.
Construction costsMaterial pricing and trade availability move.Scopes are defined before they are priced, with contingency carried.
Weather & force majeureCoastal work carries seasonal and storm exposure.Schedule float, insurance and proactive jobsite planning.
Market conditionsDemand and absorption conditions change.Tested against a range of conditions, not one favorable case.
