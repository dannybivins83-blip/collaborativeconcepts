    The handoff problem

    Why one flood turns into two projects

    Water damage work splits into mitigation (stop it, dry it) and reconstruction (put it back). Most companies in this county only do the first half — because the second half needs a contractor's licence they don't hold. So you get dried out, then you're on your own to find, vet and schedule a GC while your kitchen sits in pieces.

    Straight answers

    Questions people actually ask at 2am

    How fast can you get here?
    We answer the phone 24/7 and target same-day on-site anywhere in Palm Beach County. Call rather than fill in the form if water is actively running — a form can't tell us how urgent it is.
    Do I have to use the company my insurance recommends?
    No. In Florida you choose who repairs your property. Carriers often have preferred vendor programmes, and you're free to use them — but you're not obligated to. It's your claim and your home.
    Why does the rebuild matter so much?
    Because it's usually the bigger half of the cost and the whole reason the job drags on. Drying takes days; rebuilding takes weeks. A mitigation-only company hands you off at exactly the point the hard part starts. We hold the contractor's licence, so we don't.
    Are you licensed to handle mold?
    We remove mold-damaged material under our State Certified General Contractor licence (CGC059211), which Florida law recognises for this work when performed within the scope of contracting. We don't hold ourselves out as DBPR-licensed mold remediators, and we'll say so plainly. If your situation calls for an independent mold assessment, we'll tell you that too — and you're entitled to get competitive bids on any repair we propose.
    Will you find out why it happened?
    Yes — and this is the part we're unusually good at. Our core trade is waterproofing and concrete restoration: building envelopes, balconies, slabs, negative-side sealing. If water is getting in through the structure rather than a burst pipe, replacing the drywall just buys you a few months.
    Do you work with condo associations and HOAs?
    Regularly. Multi-unit water events, common-area intrusion and chronic envelope leaks are core work for us, and we can produce the documentation and scopes a board needs to make a decision.
    What does it cost?
    It depends on how much got wet, what it's made of, and how long it sat — anyone who quotes a water-damage job over the phone without seeing it is guessing. What we will do is tell you honestly whether it's a claim or a cash job, and put the whole scope in writing before we start.
