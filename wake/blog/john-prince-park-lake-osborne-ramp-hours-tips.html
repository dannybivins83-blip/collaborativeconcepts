John Prince Park & Lake Osborne: ramp, hours & local tips

Everything you need to launch on Lake Osborne from John Prince Park — the ramp, parking, hours and local tips — plus the easy way to skip the ramp entirely.

DBBy Danny Bivins · MasterCraft X30 owner & Palm Beach charter captain

John Prince Park is the front door to Lake Osborne, and it's one of the oldest and largest parks in the Palm Beach County system. If you're launching a boat, learning to wakesurf, or just planning a day on the Chain of Lakes, this is the spot you'll come to know. Here's the local rundown on the ramp, the hours and the little things that make a launch day smooth instead of stressful.

Where it is

John Prince Park wraps the western and southern shores of Lake Osborne between Lake Worth Beach and Lantana, just off Congress Avenue and minutes from I-95. That central location is a big part of why the lake is such a reliable spot — you can be on the water within a few minutes of getting off the highway. Check current details and any closures on the official John Prince Park page before you go.

The boat ramp and parking

The park has boat ramp access onto Lake Osborne with dedicated trailer parking nearby. A few local tips that save headaches:

  • Arrive early on weekends. The ramp and trailer lot fill up fast on nice Saturdays and holidays. A weekday or an early launch means no waiting.
  • Have your boat ready before you back down. Load gear, remove tie-downs and prep in the staging area, not on the ramp — the regulars will thank you.
  • Watch the wind forecast, not just the sky. Lake Osborne glasses off in light wind; a breezy afternoon can chop it up. Mornings are your friend.

Hours and what else is there

County parks like John Prince generally open around sunrise and close around sunset, with the campground on its own schedule — always confirm current hours on the park's official page, since they change seasonally. Beyond the ramp, the park is a genuine day-use destination: picnic pavilions, a lakeside campground, fishing spots, walking and biking paths, and playgrounds. It's easy to make the non-boaters in your group happy on shore while the riders are out on the water.

Know before you launch

Lake Osborne has idle-speed and manatee-protection zones near the ramp and in the connecting canals, and tow sports have their own rules. Before you launch, review FWC's boating and tow-sports rules: you'll need an observer or an approved mirror while towing, tow sports are daylight-only, and anyone born on or after January 1, 1988 needs a Florida boater education card to operate. Following the zones keeps the lake calm, safe and open for everyone.

Where to stay and stage

Coming from out of town or getting an early start? The Holiday Inn Express Lantana is only about five minutes from the ramp, which makes a sunrise launch painless. And if you're a boat owner who'd rather not deal with a trailer at all, keep in mind that ramp mornings, maintenance and storage add up — sometimes the math points toward selling or chartering instead.

Frequently asked

Does John Prince Park have a boat ramp for Lake Osborne?+

Yes. John Prince Park has boat ramp access onto Lake Osborne with dedicated trailer parking, located off Congress Avenue between Lake Worth Beach and Lantana, minutes from I-95.

What are John Prince Park's hours?+

County parks generally open around sunrise and close around sunset, with the campground on its own schedule. Hours change seasonally, so confirm current times on the official Palm Beach County park page before you go.

When is the best time to launch on Lake Osborne?+

Early mornings, especially weekdays. The ramp is less crowded and the lake is glassiest in light morning wind before the afternoon breeze picks up.

Can I ride Lake Osborne without owning a boat?+

Yes. A captained charter launches on the Chain of Lakes and handles the boat, fuel, gear and cleanup, so you skip the ramp entirely. Call or text (561) 475-8615 to book.
