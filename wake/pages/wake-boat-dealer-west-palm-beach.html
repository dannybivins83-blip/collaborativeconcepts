Where West Palm Beach gets on the water

Currie Park boat ramp (N Flagler Dr) puts you straight onto the Intracoastal / Lake Worth Lagoon in the heart of WPB. Phil Foster Park / Blue Heron in Riviera Beach is the go-to launch just north. For flat-water wakesurfing, Lake Osborne at John Prince Park is the county's home wake water — a short tow south. And Shark Wake Park 561 keeps a whole community of riders looking for their own boat.

We ride these waters, and every boat we help you buy, sell or service gets set up for them.

Getting to the showroom from WPB

From downtown West Palm Beach, take I-95 south to the Boynton Beach Blvd exit — the dealership sits on N Federal Hwy, roughly 15–20 minutes from the WPB waterfront. Call or text ahead and they'll have the boats you're interested in ready when you pull up.
