Good to know

Charter FAQ

Do I need a fishing license?+

For saltwater trips, you're covered under the captain's charter license — no individual license needed. For freshwater lake trips, we'll let you know in advance if a Florida freshwater license applies and how to grab one.

When is lobster season?+

Florida's two-day sport season — "mini-season" — is always the last consecutive Wednesday and Thursday of July, which makes it July 29–30, 2026. The regular season then runs August 6 through March 31. Key West is in Monroe County, which has its own stricter rules — a 6-per-person daily bag limit, where most of Florida allows 12. Mini-season is the busiest two days of the year on the water, so ask early.

What's included?+

Rods, reels, tackle and bait, coolers and ice, USCG safety gear, and a captain who puts you on the fish. For cruises, bring your own snacks and drinks — we'll handle the rest.

Where do we launch?+

It depends on your trip — offshore runs go out an inlet, inshore and cruises stay in the Intracoastal, and freshwater trips launch from a local lake ramp. We'll confirm the meeting spot when you book.

How many people can come?+

Up to 6 passengers aboard the Sea Hunt. Great for families, small groups, bachelor/bachelorette outings and first-timers.

What should I bring?+

Sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, a light jacket for sunset runs, and any snacks or drinks you'd like. We provide the boat, gear, safety equipment and local know-how.

Do you keep the fish we catch?+

Your catch is yours — within legal limits and seasons. We'll clean and bag your keepers at the dock so they're ready for the grill.
