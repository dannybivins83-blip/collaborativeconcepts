On a $120,000 wake boat, that's not a risk worth taking to save a few dollars on a service call.

We do it the opposite way — out loud and on the record:

  • We publish our reviews — a 4.8-star record built over 25 years, not hidden behind a burner phone number.
  • We publish our certifications — factory training on the engines and systems in your boat, not "I've watched a few videos."
  • We publish our address — a real Boynton Beach shop you can walk into, with people whose names you'll learn.

Why owners choose a shop over a stranger

A wake boat isn't a jon boat — the drivetrain, ballast plumbing and surf electronics are specialized, and a wrong guess is expensive. A factory-trained shop with a real service history, real reviews, and a real address is the cheapest insurance you can buy on a boat this valuable. The bargain "guy with a truck" almost never is.

Straight answers

Will you service my boat even though I didn't buy it from you?

Absolutely. Whether you bought your Malibu, Nautique, Moomba, Axis, Centurion, Supra or MasterCraft here, from a dealer that has since closed, or private-party — you're welcome. Most of the boats we service now belong to owners whose dealer left the area.

Are your techs actually trained on my brand?

Yes. Our service partner's inboard technicians are factory-trained on the engines that power wake boats — Ilmor, Indmar, PCM and Crusader — and on ballast and surf systems across brands. It's a full dealer-grade service department, not a general marine handyman.

Do you really come to the dock?

Dockside and mobile service runs from the Keys to Stuart, covering all of Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade. Give us your zip code on the form and we'll confirm scheduling and any mobile-call details. Bigger jobs (gelcoat, canvas, major engine work) are usually done at the Boynton Beach shop.

How much does service cost?

It depends on the boat, the hours and what it needs — but you always get an honest estimate and your approval before any work begins. No surprise invoices, no work you didn't sign off on.

Who actually does the work?

Palm Beach Wake Boats is an independent local marketing site. The service itself is performed by our authorized service partner's licensed shop in Boynton Beach — a family-owned business with a 4.8-star reputation built over 25 years. We connect you; they do the wrenching.
