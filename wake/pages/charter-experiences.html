Good to know

Do I need any experience to wakesurf or wakeboard?

None at all. Most first-timers are up and riding the same day — the inboard wave and a patient captain make it easy. We coach kids and adults from never-ever to advanced.

How many people can come?

Tell us your group size when you book and we'll confirm what works for the day and the water. Great for small families up to bigger party groups — we'll plan it around you.

Where do we launch?

Right here in the Lantana / Lake Worth area — Lake Osborne, Lake Ida (Delray), or the Intracoastal, depending on conditions and what you want to do. We'll confirm the meet spot when we book.

What should we bring?

Swimsuits, a towel, sunscreen, and whatever snacks or drinks you like. We provide the boat, captain, boards, tubes, ropes, and life jackets.

What if the weather's bad?

Safety first — if conditions aren't right we'll reschedule you to another day, no hassle.
