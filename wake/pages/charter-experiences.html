How does splitting the cost work?+

You pick a charter (one flat price for the whole boat) and how many are coming. We split that total across your crew so everyone sees their share. You reserve the boat, and we text you a secure link to pay your share and collect everyone else's — no chasing friends for cash.

Do I need any experience to wakesurf or wakeboard?+

None at all. Most first-timers are up and riding the same day — the inboard wave and a patient captain make it easy. We coach kids and adults from never-ever to advanced.

Can you do birthdays, bachelorette parties, tubing or a grill-out?+

Absolutely — that's most of what we do. Sandbar grill-outs, the Hilton-pool canal cruise through Stub Lake, birthday & bachelorette groups, tubing for all ages, sunset cruises. Tell us what you want when you book and we'll build the day around your crew.

Where do we launch — and can you come to us?+

Lake Osborne (Lantana), Lake Ida (Delray), or the Intracoastal (Boynton), depending on what you want to do. We can also launch from your dock or ramp — just pick "We come to you" when you book.

What should we bring?+

Swimsuits, a towel, sunscreen, and whatever snacks or drinks you like. We provide the boat, captain, boards, tubes, ropes, life jackets, fuel and ice.
