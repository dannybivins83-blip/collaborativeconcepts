Quick FL tow-sports rules

• Observer or wide-angle mirror required while towing
• Riders need a USCG-approved non-inflatable life jacket
• No towing from ½ hr after sunset to ½ hr before sunrise
• Wakesurfing requires an inboard / V-drive — never an outboard or sterndrive

Straight answers

How is the estimate calculated?+

From current and recent South Florida listings, adjusted for model tier, age and engine hours. A realistic asking range, not an appraisal — your written offer follows a quick photo/condition review.

What does consignment cost me?+

Typically 10–15% of the sale price, including prep, secured storage, professional demos, financing arrangement and title transfer. You approve the asking price — nothing sells below it without your OK.

How fast do wake boats sell?+

Priced-right used wake boats are scarce — fewer than 30 used MasterCrafts are typically listed statewide. Clean 2018–2023 surf boats in the $70K–$150K band move fastest.

Eligible if it's not a MasterCraft?+

Yes. Malibu, Nautique, Axis, Moomba, Supra, Centurion, Tige — valuation, consignment and service cover every inboard wake brand.

Who actually buys / sells the boat?+

Palm Beach Wake Boats is an independent local marketing company. Transactions are completed by our authorized dealer partner's licensed sales operation in Boynton Beach — a family-owned, 4.8-star dealership. We connect you; they close it properly.
