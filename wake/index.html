Lake Ida (Delray Beach): public ramp at Lake Ida West Park; go weekday mornings, weekends get busy. We're local. We ride here. And every boat we help sell or buy gets set up for these waters — not some dealer's lake three hours away.

Quick FL tow-sports rules

• Observer or wide-angle mirror required while towing
• Riders need a USCG-approved non-inflatable life jacket
• No towing from ½ hr after sunset to ½ hr before sunrise
• Wakesurfing requires an inboard/V-drive boat — never surf behind an outboard or sterndrive

Straight answers

How is the instant estimate calculated?

From current and recent South Florida wake-boat listings, adjusted for model tier, age and engine hours. It's a realistic asking-price range, not an appraisal — your written offer follows a quick photo/condition review with the dealer's sales desk.

What does consignment cost me?

Dealer consignment in this market typically runs 10–15% of the sale price, and includes prep, secured storage, professional demos to qualified buyers, financing arrangement and title transfer. You approve the asking price — nothing sells below it without your OK.

How fast do wake boats sell right now?

Priced-right used wake boats are scarce in South Florida — fewer than 30 used MasterCrafts are typically listed in the entire state. Clean 2018–2023 surf boats in the $70K–$150K band are the fastest movers.
